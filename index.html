Our Story

Malenadu Kitchen started as a small home kitchen in Malleshwaram, serving lunch to five regulars a day. Today, we still cook the same recipes, in the same order, the way they were taught to us.

No shortcuts, no pre-mixed masalas — every spice is roasted and ground the same morning it's used.
